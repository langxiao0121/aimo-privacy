# Aimo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 28, 2026_

Aimo is a private journal for pet owners. This policy explains, in plain language, what Aimo does with your information — and what it doesn't.

---

## What Aimo collects

Aimo stores the following on your device, and syncs it to your personal iCloud:

- **Pet records you create**: text, dates, places you type in, weight entries, traits, time capsules, letters.
- **Photos you attach to records**: stored as references to your existing Photos library plus a small sandbox copy for permanence inside Aimo.
- **Health events and reminders**: vaccines, deworming, vet visits, and any local notification schedules tied to them.
- **App preferences**: weight unit, toggles, and similar settings.

Everything above lives in your **iCloud Private Database** (your account, your device). It is not a backend we operate. We cannot read it.

---

## What Aimo does on your device only

- **Photo description suggestions** use Apple's on-device Vision framework. Your photos are never uploaded for analysis.
- **Photo location lookup** ("where this was taken") uses the GPS your photo already contains, plus Apple's CLGeocoder. The lookup happens through Apple's services on your device. You can turn this off in *Quiet things → Looked at*.
- **Reminders** use iOS local notifications. They are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

---

## What Aimo does NOT collect

- **No analytics.** Aimo does not include Firebase, Google Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or any other analytics SDK.
- **No advertising.** No ad networks, no tracking pixels, no IDFA use.
- **No third-party cloud LLMs.** Photo captions, search, and any AI features run on your device using Apple frameworks.
- **No account on our servers.** Aimo has no login. Your iCloud account is the only identity.
- **No social features.** Aimo does not share, post, or publish anything on your behalf.

---

## What you control

- **Turn off Reminders** — *Quiet things → Heard → Reminders*.
- **Turn off photo reading** — *Quiet things → Looked at → Read photos for context*.
- **Turn off photo location lookup** — *Quiet things → Looked at → Look up where photos were*.
- **Export your book** — *Quiet things → About them → Keep a copy of [pet]'s book* gives you a self-contained `.aimobook` file.
- **Delete a pet** — removes that pet and all of its records, photos, and reminders from this device and iCloud.
- **Delete the app** — iOS removes the local copy. To remove the iCloud copy, delete the data inside iCloud Settings → Aimo, or sign out of iCloud.

---

## Children

Aimo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
